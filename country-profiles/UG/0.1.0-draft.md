# Uganda country profile — controlled draft

Effective date: unset pending review  
Owner: to be assigned by the deploying organization

## Scope

This profile is a configuration and validation checklist for a Uganda pilot. It is not legal advice, regulatory certification, a clinical guideline, or permission to process patient data. The deploying clinic/network must identify its legal role(s), processing purposes, counterparties, hosting locations and actual data flows.

## Privacy and data governance gates

1. Confirm the controller/processor/data collector roles for ODEI, each facility, hosting vendors and integration partners.
2. Confirm applicable registration obligations and obtain current PDPO guidance/certificates before live personal-data processing. PDPO material describes registration duties for data collectors, processors and controllers; obtain a current determination for each legal entity and processing purpose.
3. Maintain a processing-purpose inventory, data categories, data-subject transparency notices, lawful basis, retention schedule, security safeguards, data-subject request handling and incident/breach process.
4. Assess every cross-border transfer and hosting arrangement against current Ugandan law and PDPO requirements before choosing a region or provider. Do not infer adequacy from a provider's marketing statement.
5. Approve access controls, confidentiality, processor contracts, subprocessor inventory, backup location, key ownership and deletion/retention controls.
6. Obtain qualified Ugandan legal/privacy review and record evidence, reviewer, date and approval.

## Clinical and professional governance gates

- Validate facility service scope, staff registration/licensure and role-based scope of practice.
- Obtain current Ministry of Health guidance and facility policy for EMR, health information exchange, reporting and interoperability.
- Approve locally applicable clinical protocols, formularies, vaccine schedules, laboratory catalogues/reference ranges, critical-result escalation and referral pathways with the responsible clinical leads.
- Validate national identifier use and patient identity matching; do not make a national identifier mandatory unless legally authorized and operationally appropriate.
- Verify all terminology, language, timezone, date/number formats and patient-facing notices.

## Financial and operational configuration

- Configure UGX as the pilot accounting currency only after finance-owner approval.
- Configure facility tariffs, tax treatment, payer contracts, cash controls, refund thresholds, journal mapping and claim formats from approved source documents.
- Configure payment channels and reconciliation only after testing with the actual provider and settlement account.
- Set facility-specific service hours, escalation contacts, downtime workflow, RPO/RTO, backup schedule and support targets.

## Interoperability

Select and pin the FHIR release and implementation guide for each actual integration partner. Confirm endpoint ownership, authentication, consent/legal basis, terminology mappings, test environment, error handling and conformance evidence. Do not advertise national interoperability until the relevant counterpart has been tested and accepted.
